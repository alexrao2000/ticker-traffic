# Playcast Script

As you can see each lane represents a different ticker. Most of them are stocks but we also have BTC just for fun.

Collisions cause losses. 

Your vehicle is slow when changing lanes to add some risk

You can step on the brakes or speed up.

You have a ton of info on your dashboard to calculate your moves.

Earnings and losses are split up for your analysis.

You can stop on the shoulder, in other words, sell everything.
